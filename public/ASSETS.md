## Textures (`public/textures/`)

Generated by Adnan in the Gemini app (Nano Banana) on his Google AI Pro
subscription, which is free and produces no API charge. Full resolution
originals live in `raw-assets/textures/`, which is gitignored and never
served. `scripts/optimise-assets.mjs` produces the files below from them, so
regenerating is a matter of dropping a new file into `raw-assets/textures/`
with a matching prefix and re-running it.

| File | Source | Output | Size |
|---|---|---|---|
| `paper-grain-01.webp` | `paper-grain-01.jpg`, 4096x4096, 9.57MB | 1024x1024 webp | 10 KB |
| `social-share-01.jpg` | `social-share-01.jpg`, 1424x752, 634KB | 1200x630 jpeg | 14 KB |

The paper grain is an overlay for the warm section, drawn at low opacity over
the paper ground. The share image is the Open Graph card, sized to the exact
1200x630 every social platform reads.
